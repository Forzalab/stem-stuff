    Priming questions

    1. Right after the green check, where did your thumb go first? Was "Next" already there?
    2. After a wrong pick, did anything look like "move on" louder than "pick again"?
    3. On the long question (the Ferris wheel), could you reach the choices and Next without scrolling back up?
    4. With the Cluck sheet open, could you still see and tap Next?
    5. With the keyboard up, did the bar get in your way, and did Next come back once you were right?
    6. Did anything ever cover a choice or the line under it?
    7. At midnight, tired, would you know in under a second how to go on? Which variant felt like the app was waiting for you, not pushing you?
    8. Out of tries: does "This one comes back around." feel like a door, or like a verdict?
    9. Desktop: does the same Next feel like the same app as on the phone?
