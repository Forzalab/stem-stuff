Same in all three. The bonus is always +3, never a spin. A Redeem can't be lost: a miss costs nothing and the question returns later, still worth +3. The burst moves 14 px at most and fades; with reduced motion it is a still "+3".

Where it sits. v1 in the label row (top left area, not a control), v2 on the question card, v3 beside the Check button. None of them is a button: nothing to tap by mistake.

Priming questions

  1. Does she notice it came back before she reads the stem, and does that feel like a second chance or a test she failed before?
  2. Does "+3" read as a fixed promise she can count on?
  3. After a miss on a Redeem, does she believe nothing was taken?
  4. Is the burst where her eye already is at the moment of the right answer?
  5. Does any variant make the question feel like homework she owes?
  6. Which one would make her want the next Redeem tomorrow?
