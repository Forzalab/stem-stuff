Privacy

What we record: which pages and questions you open, which answer you pick and if it was right, how long things take, when you open Cluck or leave, taps that don't seem to work, your browser and device type, basic device facts (screen, processor cores, memory, graphics chip, data saver, motion and transparency settings), and how smoothly the page scrolls and animates (PostHog). When something breaks, you see an error code. With it we record what failed and some technical detail about your browser, only to fix that error. What you type, your answers and your notes are never in an error report. The app keeps a short log of its last steps on this device only (what loaded and how long it took, screen size and scrolling, where buttons sat, and which kind of key was pressed in which box, never the letters), and an error report includes it. We don't record what you type, and there is no screen replay. No name, no login: just a random ID on this device. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we record nothing from your device; when something breaks, our server still notes the error code and what failed, with no ID.

What Cluck gets: when you ask Cluck, it gets the question, your latest answer, the last 1500 characters of your notes, and what you type to it. After a wrong answer, Cluck may start its reply before you open it; it gets the same things then.

What stays on this device: finished questions keep your answers and the hints you opened, so they show again after a reload. Copy puts your answers, your notes and their edit history on your clipboard; they only go where you paste them.

Why: to see where questions get confusing, so we can make them clearer, and to keep the site fast on your device.

Questions, or want your data removed? Ask Tony, who shared this app with you. Please keep private info (phone numbers, passwords) out of your notes and Cluck.
