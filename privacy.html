Privacy

What we record: which pages and questions you open, which answer you pick and if it was right, how long things take, when you open Cluck or leave, taps that don't seem to work, and your browser and device type (PostHog). We don't record what you type, and there is no screen replay. No name, no login: just a random ID on this device. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we record nothing.

Why: to see where questions get confusing, so we can make them clearer.

Questions, or want your data removed? Ask Tony, who shared this app with you. Please keep private info (phone numbers, passwords) out of your notes and Cluck.
