Privacy

What we record: what you tap, pick and type in this app, and a screen replay of the visit (PostHog and Microsoft Clarity). No name, no login. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we record nothing.

Why: to see where questions get confusing, so we can make them clearer.

Questions, or want your data removed? Ask Tony, who shared this app with you. Please keep private info (phone numbers, passwords) out of your notes and Cluck.
