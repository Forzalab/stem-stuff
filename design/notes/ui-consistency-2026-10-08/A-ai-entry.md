# A. AI-mode entry points: how a student reaches Cluck on a question

Read-only audit, 2026-10-08, off `origin/main` 6f7bec5. Local `serve.py` on its own port; `/explain` and `/chat` stubbed with `page.route`; YouTube requests aborted (so the `#rot` players are blank white cards in every shot; their position and chrome are real). No real model was called, no code changed.
Harness: `design/shots/ui-audit-a-2026-10-08/shoot.mjs`; raw per-row DOM facts: `design/shots/ui-audit-a-2026-10-08/facts.json`. Shots live in `design/shots/ui-audit-a-2026-10-08/` (links below are relative to this note).

## How the states were produced

- Fixtures (3 questions in a throwaway bank, same shape as `tests/blind.pw.mjs:20-27`): **snack** (`saccharine.snack` + `original`), **non-snack keyed** (`saccharine.key` = a "wish"), **plain** (no `saccharine.key`: no wish). The repo ships no keyed problem and no snack (`problems.json`, `banks/BANK_PSY6/7.json`: 0 of 178 have `saccharine.key` or `snack`), so the Cluck UI only exists with banks supplied from outside.
- Wrong answer = tap choice a ("1.0 m/s", authored hint "QUACK. Total mass.").
- "Cap hit (local)": `localStorage["stem-wish"]` holds 5 timestamps from the last hour (`WISH_AUTO = 5`, app.js:1116-1118); `wishOnWrong` then sets `started = false` and does not call `/explain` (app.js:1137). "Cap hit (server 429)": `/explain` stubbed 429; `wishStart` flips to `started = false, capped = true` (app.js:1164).
- "Failed": `/explain` stubbed 500 (`w.failed = 500`). 503 added: `wishPaint` and `clTab` treat 404 and 503 as "Cluck is off" and hide everything (app.js:1261-1264, 1331).
- "pending": `/explain` never answers (stream stays open, label "Cluck is writing the steps…").
- Phone 393x852 (touch, mobile, DPR 2); desktop 1280x800 (`sideMQ` = min-width 720px, app.js:1665). Each state is shot as it lands, then the sheet is toggled (closed -> opened by the first visible entry, open -> closed with the X) and shot again. So "sheet open/closed" appears in both forms for every reachable state. On desktop a wrong answer lands with the sheet open (auto-open, app.js:1136, 1139) and a snack lands with it open even before answering (app.js:1509).

## Inconsistencies found, ranked by how often a student hits them

Ranking basis: phone is the primary device, and the first wrong answer on a keyed question is the commonest path into Cluck. The repo ships no keyed or snack content, so real frequency of snack vs non-snack is unknown from here; ordering is by how much of the normal path each item sits on.

1. **One destination, three doors, two titles, two fold states (every wrong answer on a keyed question).**
   - Phone snack: the only door is the orange "Similar solution steps" card with a FREE sticker (row 5, `phone-snack-wrong-done-closed.png`; chip hidden by app.js:1267). The explanation of the student's own mistake is not named anywhere on that screen.
   - Phone non-snack: an orange chip "Show Cluck's steps" under the hint (row 18). Desktop: the sheet auto-opens (row 34), and once closed the chip and the card both come back (row 33).
   - The sheet's head bar says "Similar solution steps" on a snack and "Cluck" otherwise (app.js:1341; rows 6 vs 19), even when the chip opened it (row 12). The code comment at app.js:1341 says the card's name shows "when the card opened it", but the test is `has.steps`, not the opener.
   - Opened from the phone snack card, the sheet shows Cluck's text with the reference solution open under it (rows 4, 6; `clOpen("steps")` sets `cl.fold = true`, app.js:1316). Desktop auto-open shows the same content with the fold closed (rows 32, 34; `clOpen("explain")`).
2. **The phone "one entry" rule is conditional, so the screen shows one or two buttons depending on a request outcome (phone snack: failure, local cap, server cap).**
   - Healthy: card only (row 5). Failed, local cap, server cap: card at Y 92 plus a second orange chip at Y 555, far apart, both opening the same sheet (rows 7, 11, 13; app.js:1267 `w.started && !w.failed`).
   - Tapping the chip in the cap state makes the chip disappear and the sheet is titled with the card's name (row 12).
   - After a server 429 the chip still reads "Explain my mistake" and fires another `/explain` that 429s again (row 14; `facts.json` `explainCalls` 1 -> 2). Inside the sheet the ask field still shows "5 left" but is disabled (`phone-snack-cap-429-open.png`; app.js:1381-1384).
3. **Desktop with the sheet closed contradicts the phone rule.** A snack shows both "Similar solution steps" (right column, Y 596) and "Show Cluck's steps" (left, Y 625) at once (rows 31, 33, 35, 39, 41; app.js:1266 `el.hidden = w.open` on desktop vs 1267 on phone). While the sheet is open on desktop the chip is hidden (row 34), but with a cap hit the "Explain my mistake" chip stays visible next to an open sheet that shows the steps (row 40).
4. **Cap hit gives no signal until the student asks.**
   - Local cap (5 auto-starts an hour, app.js:1116-1118, 1137): the chip is the normal "Explain my mistake" (rows 11, 23, 39, 51). It still works when tapped (rows 12, 24: Cluck text arrives), so the client cap only removes the auto-start.
   - Server cap 429: only now the sheet says "Cluck is out of wishes for this hour. Try again later." (rows 14, 26, 42, 54; app.js:1233). The chip label does not change.
5. **Failure copy and visibility differ by status.** 500: the chip becomes "Didn't load. Tap to try again" (rows 7, 20) and the sheet repeats it with an inline "Try again" (rows 8, 21; app.js:1231-1235). 404 or 503: every Cluck control vanishes silently (rows 9, 22, 37, 50; app.js:1261-1264, 1331); on a phone snack the card is then the only help and shows just the reference solution (row 10).
6. **"Explain" names three different things.** The desktop `#rot` column header is a speech bubble plus "Explain" over the brainrot videos (brainrot.js:79; rows 29-56, e.g. `desktop-nonsnack-wrong-done-closed.png`), the sheet's aria-label is "Explain" (app.js:1289), and the chip says "Explain my mistake" (app.js:1268). On desktop with the sheet closed the "Explain" column holds no explanation (row 46). On phone, `#rot` floats at its corner, but is parked to an edge tab exactly in the two-button states (rows 7, 11, 13 vs rows 3, 5; brainrot.js:166), so the AI buttons move the videos.
7. **The hint row looks like Cluck but is not an entry.** The authored QUACK line carries the duck icon (app.js:1091) and sits directly above the chip (rows 5, 18), but it is static text. A keyed-less question shows the duck hint and no Cluck door at all (rows 28, 56; `wish` needs `saccharine.key`, serve.py:325-326).
8. **Key formulas is the stable one.** It shows in every keyed state and device (rows 1-26, 29-54), between the answer and the hint row (app.js:576). Its gate is `formulas` (app.js:569) while `#rot` is gated on `wish` (app.js:342), so a question can have one without the other (code only, no shot). It is not an AI entry; listed so the synthesizer knows it never changes.

Not covered: level-3 folded card (after a first-try solve of a snack's original; needs reward state, app.js:1509); the follow-up chat turns (`/chat` stub was installed, never hit); multi-part and fill-in kinds (B's scope); toasts and notes (C's scope); WebKit.

Candidate rule from A's view: one named Cluck door per question, with the same label and place on every device and state, and the exam-steps card becoming content inside Cluck's sheet instead of a second door.
