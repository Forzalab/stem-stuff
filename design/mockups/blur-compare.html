Sticker blur check: before vs after

Each row shows the same sticker twice. BEFORE is the app today. AFTER is the fix: the sticker card still leans, but the words stay straight, so Windows can draw them sharp.

How to check (on the Windows laptop):
  1. Open this file in the browser you use for the app (double-click it; no internet needed).
  2. Look at the words at 100% zoom (Ctrl + 0), then 125% and 150% (Ctrl + +). Windows display scaling of 125% or 150% counts too.
  3. In each row, are the AFTER words as sharp as normal page text? Are the BEFORE words softer or fuzzy?
