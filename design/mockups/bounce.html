Bounce

  1. 1 Native. Nothing added. overscroll-behavior: contain keeps whatever the platform draws (iOS rubber-band, Android stretch), nothing on desktop Chrome.
  2. 2 Rubber-band. The content follows the finger, damped by x·d·c/(d + c·x), c = 0.55, then springs back (WAAPI). Drag with a finger or the mouse at the top or bottom.
  3. 3 Bump. No finger follow. A wheel or fling that reaches the edge plays one short out-and-back, scaled by speed.
  4. Reduced motion: no transform in any variant.
