Exam version: a 2.0 kg puck at 4.0 m/s hits a resting 2.0 kg puck. They leave at 30° above and 60° below the line. Find v1.

Worked solution

  1. Momentum is kept along x and along y.
  2. y: 0 = v1 sin 30° − v2 sin 60°
  3. x: 4.0 = v1 cos 30° + v2 cos 60°
  4. Solve the pair: v1 = 3.46 m/s
